Michigan Public Health Institute (MPHI) is a nonprofit public health organization whose primary customer is the Michigan Department of Health and Human Services. MPHI is seeking a Senior .NET Developer to lead the design, development, maintenance, and modernization of web-based applications supporting critical public health initiatives. This role requires a highly experienced full stack developer with deep expertise in traditional MVC application development, .NET Core, Entity Framework, C#, and SQL Server. The ideal candidate has thrived in consulting environments, successfully balancing multiple concurrent projects while providing technical leadership, mentoring less experienced developers, and collaborating directly with clients.

Design, maintain, and manage SQL Server databases.

Mentor developers, conduct code reviews, and establish development standards.
Perform solution risk analysis and recommend best practices.
Gather technical requirements and collaborate directly with clients and stakeholders.
Create and maintain technical documentation.
Troubleshoot and provide technical support for applications and users.
Lead initiatives focused on technology modernization and process improvement.
