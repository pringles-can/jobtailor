Senior Java Engineer — Batch Processing & Data Integration
Midwest Mutual Insurance | Lansing, MI (Hybrid)

About the role

Our policy administration platform processes millions of premium and claims
transactions nightly through a large suite of Oracle batch jobs. We are looking
for a senior engineer who is comfortable in legacy enterprise environments and
can make long-running batch processing faster, more reliable, and easier to
support.

This is a hands-on maintenance and modernization role. You will spend your time
in Java, PL/SQL, and shell tooling, working closely with our operations team and
with business analysts in underwriting and billing.

Responsibilities

- Maintain, troubleshoot, and tune a portfolio of 150+ nightly Oracle batch jobs
- Diagnose and resolve production failures, including on-call rotation
- Improve batch runtimes and reduce the nightly processing window
- Modernize legacy job implementations while preserving exact billing behavior
- Build and maintain ETL processes that exchange data with external carriers
- Work with EDI and other flat-file formats from partner organizations
- Write and optimize complex SQL against large Oracle schemas
- Partner directly with business subject matter experts to translate
  requirements into technical specifications
- Support a Waterfall SDLC with formal release and change-control processes

Required qualifications

- 7+ years of professional software engineering experience
- Strong Java and object-relational mapping experience (Hibernate or similar)
- Deep SQL and Oracle experience, including performance tuning
- Experience with batch processing and scheduled job frameworks
- Experience with ETL and file-based data integration
- Demonstrated ability to improve performance of existing systems
- Comfort working in and maintaining legacy codebases
- Experience supporting production systems

Preferred qualifications

- Insurance, healthcare, or other regulated industry background
- EDI or standardized data interchange formats
- Scripting experience (Bash, Groovy, Perl)
- Experience mentoring junior engineers
- Experience documenting undocumented legacy processes

What we offer

Stable, long-tenured team. Strong work-life balance with predictable release
cadence. Full benefits, pension, and tuition reimbursement.
